# At most two assignments from an informative arithmetic cycle

**Status: research proof draft; independent mathematical review pending.** Automated evidence is scoped below and does not certify the complete argument.

**Status. Written graph proof, exact algebraic root bound and differential tests.** Let L be a finite positive value cap. Fix a program interval, the exact entry registers, permitted operations, output obligations and fixed side operands. Each unknown denotes the value of a chosen write. An observation has the form A(xᵢ) op B(xⱼ) = t. Each arm is a fixed sequence of additions, ordered differences, multiplications and ordered exact divisions with a positive known side operand. An empty arm is allowed. Terminal t is a specified positive value, including any internal target required to have been visited. Constants must be legally available at their uses. Auxiliary equalities between two otherwise unknown arm values without a specified terminal are outside this statement.

Make a multigraph on unknowns with one edge per observation. Self-loops, parallel edges, and aliased numerical values are permitted. For a connected component choose a root and a spanning tree. Each edge gives an invertible fractional linear transformation over the rational projective line. In particular the oriented terminal maps left-arm value u to the right-arm value by t−u, u−t, t/u or u/t respectively. Conjugate by the arm maps on each side. Addition of a constant, multiplication by a positive constant and reciprocal with positive numerator all have nonzero determinant. The same holds under composition and inversion.

The unique tree path from the root gives a transformation Pᵢ at each vertex. For every edge i→j with transform E, a legal root x must be fixed by Pⱼ⁻¹ E Pᵢ. If at least one of these products is different from the projective identity, there are at most two candidate root values, regardless of word length or the number of unknowns. For each root the tree uniquely determines all other values. Thus at most two tuples suffice, or at most 2v scalar helper entries for v vertices. Matrix operations are polynomial in the encoded template size: coefficient bit lengths grow at most additively in path lengths and operand bit lengths up to a constant per matrix multiplication. Finding the topology and decoding a physical schedule are separate costs.

**Proof.** A finite fixed point of (a x+b)/(c x+d) satisfies c x²+(d−a)x−b=0. This is a nonzero polynomial precisely when the matrix is nonscalar. Enumerate exact bounded integral roots, intersect lists across informative cycles, propagate by the tree, then replay every intermediate under positivity, cap and exact division. Every legal assignment survives. Every returned assignment satisfies the numerical observations. A component with only identity cycles can still have arithmetic restrictions and requires a separate anchor or root domain. The implementation reports that condition explicitly. The proof uses standard gain-graph balance and elementary algebra; novelty of these underlying facts is not claimed. [Gain graphs](https://people.math.binghamton.edu/zaslav/Tpapers/bg1.jctb1989.pdf)

**Physical corollary.** Take any improving finite-register program in this family, with fixed entry state, destination mapping for outgoing live values and required target visits, and include every unknown value site covered by the claim. Filtering those sites by their corresponding projected menu retains that very program and its instruction count. Thus the menu is complete for these sites and this fixed template. The output of the numerical solver alone supplies neither a source of a helper nor a register assignment. Additional free writes or an unobserved helper require their own model. A witnessed chain can supply a helper only if its seed is live and the chain's operations and physical scheduling costs are included.

**Sharp examples.** The observations x+y=5 and xy=6 allow the two ordered tuples (2,3) and (3,2). Two copies of x+y=L admit L−1 ordered assignments, so connectivity and the existence of a cycle alone cannot bound the menu. For x→2/x→2/(2/x), rational simplification gives x, yet x=3 is illegal at the first division. Even a uniquely determined value can be impossible to produce from the actual live registers.
